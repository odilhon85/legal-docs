Privacy Policy

Effective date: 01.09.2026  |  Last updated: 01.09.2026

Buxgalteriya Schyotlari (also known as “Schyot”) (“the App”) is an educational mobile application for learning accounting terms and provodka, developed by Islomov Odilhon Rustamovich ("Developer", "we", "us", "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects (if any), how it is used, and your rights regarding that data. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

Buxgalteriya Schyotlari is designed with a privacy-first approach. The App works fully offline and does not connect to any external servers operated by us. All data related to your learning progress, tests, and preferences is stored locally on your device. We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Data Type Purpose Storage
Learning progress Track completed modules (e.g., Tez Yodla, Soz Topish, Test Qilish) per batch. Local database on your device.
Test results Store test scores (total questions, correct answers, timestamp) for batches and full tests. Local database on your device.
App preferences Save settings such as audio enabled/disabled, theme mode (System/Light/Dark), text size, and provodka group completion status. Local storage on your device.
Game-related data Data related to the interactive “Buxgalter o‘yini” (accounting game) played inside the App via a local WebView. Local storage on your device.

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number).
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not access contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID).
  • We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics, etc.).
  • We do not send your data to any external server or third party.

Google Play Billing handles all payment-related information. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card details or billing address. Any data processed by Google is governed by their own privacy policy and terms.

4. Children’s Privacy

Buxgalteriya Schyotlari is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used this App, all data exists only on that device and can be deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All data is stored locally on your device for as long as you keep the App installed.
  • We do not retain any of your data on external servers, so there is no server-side deletion process required.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Clear App Data via Device Settings

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Buxgalteriya Schyotlari.
  3. Tap Storage → Clear Data. This deletes all local data including progress, test results, and settings.

Option 2: Uninstall the App

Uninstalling Buxgalteriya Schyotlari from your device will permanently remove all local data, including learning progress, test results, preferences, and game-related data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — all information is stored on your device and can be viewed within the App.
  • Delete your data — uninstall the App or clear its data via Android Settings.
  • Control permissions — manage any requested permissions at any time through Android Settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
